Maison Veloura

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Maison Veloura game ("the app") uses. The app is published by William Pitot ("we"). Contact: risklinedev@gmail.com.

The short version

  • No account and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email or contacts.
  • Your game progress stays on your device.
  • Ads are optional and rewarded. They are provided by Google AdMob, which collects some device data to show and measure ads, as described below.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress, settings, the record of your purchases and a small local diagnostics log are stored on your device only. We do not receive them. If you choose to use "Share diagnostics", you decide whether to send that log to us; it contains game events, not personal information.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

When you choose to watch a rewarded ad, the app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google may collect and process information such as your IP address (used to estimate an approximate location), device and advertising identifiers, ad interactions, and crash and performance data, to deliver, measure and improve ads and to prevent fraud. Learn more in Google's policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, we ask for your consent through Google's consent message before ads are requested, and you can change your choice at any time from Privacy settings in the Maison tab. On iOS, your device's advertising identifier is not available to us unless you allow tracking.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We receive a confirmation that a purchase was made, never your payment information. See Apple's privacy policy at apple.com/legal/privacy.

Notifications

If you allow them, notifications are scheduled locally on your device (for example, when a drop is ready). No data is sent to us.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Because we do not hold personal data on our side, most requests concern Google's advertising data: you can change your ad consent in the app, reset or limit your advertising identifier in iOS Settings, and contact Google. For any question, write to risklinedev@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
