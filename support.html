Support

Questions, a bug, or a purchase that did not arrive? Write to risklinedev@gmail.com. We answer within a few days.

Restore the Founder's Atelier

Open the Maison tab, find the Founder's Atelier card and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account as for the original purchase.

Is my progress saved?

Your Maison is saved automatically on your device. There is no account. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

Ads

Ads are always optional: you choose to watch a short ad to receive a reward. There are no forced or interstitial ads. In the European Economic Area and the UK, you can change your ad consent at any time from Privacy settings at the bottom of the Maison tab.

Refunds

Purchases are processed by Apple. To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
